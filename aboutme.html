About Me

Hello, my name is Denis, I study Media Computer Science (since 2020) at the Hochschule Bremen in Germany.

As already mentioned, I study Media Computer Science at the Hochschule Bremen in Germany. I decided for this bachelor’s degree because I was always invested into media design, but always wanted to learn programming and web development. Additionally, I work at my university at the department for digitalization for over 2 years now, where I am responsible for many different technicalities within my university.

When you study media computer science, it is basically a mix of everything that happens in the digital world on a visual and programming level. You learn to use the most important tools of the Adobe Cloud Software Palette (such as Photoshop, Illustrator etc.).

In terms of pure programming you start with Java and Python. Additionally to that while learning HTML, CSS, you will also learn programming in JavaScript and the additional variations of it.
