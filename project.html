neurodiversify.tech

We as a group came up with neurodiversify.tech during our time in the Interdisciplinary Lab (iLab). Neurodiversify.tech is a website that provides guidance in multiple ways for any kind of people, that needs to create content for a neurodivergent audience. Neurodivergent is an umbrella term covering multiple forms of "being different" like ADHD, ASD, and Dyslexia. Our website is aiming to contain guidance for diagnoses such as Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) and Dyslexia. Addtionally, we want to make aware why these people are sidelined and excluded and why everyone should care in his daily life. The presented minimal viable product that will be displayed aims to guide how to create content for Attention Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). Future expansions of the website shall include Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) and Dyslexia. We also want to make sure our website is functioning without any problems and is usable for any device you are using.

My projects tasks for neurodiversify.tech

When I first joined the challenge, I directly communicated my skills based on my study background. Pretty early I was involved in designing the website as a visual prototype. Later on in the development phase, I was partly responsible for the implementation of content and needed visual changes.

While designing and creating the website, one responsibility was to ensure a neurodivergent friendly interface, mainly due to the reason it is important to practice what you teach or present others. In addition to that, I tried to help my team members on a technical level, who were not into development.

While working on the project and going through different cycles of the project, feedback implementation is key. Most of the concerns were the choice of colors, font sizes and typography spacing. Addtionally, it is important to maintain the standards to ensure the purpose of the project is still on track.
