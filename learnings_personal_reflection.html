Personal Reflection & Learnings

This section of my portfolio is all about the things I personally learned during my semester abroad in the the iLab. But I will also reflect on the experiences I had during the iLab and what kind of conclusions I personally drew.

Learnings

Throughout my whole time in the iLab I got to learn a lot of new things, but I also got the chance to improve on specific skills. I personally think, both of these kinds of experiences will definitely help me in the future, and it was great to do these things, I normally would not at my university and my usual study programm.

Presentation Skills

Presenting in front of people was always a kind of struggle for me, even though I had a good understanding of my presentation topics. During the iLab, the constant tasks and processes of presenting helped me a lot to gain more confidence while presenting. From my point of view, I would rate this as one of the best things I learned while being in the iLab.

Working in a team with different backgrounds

Another important skill I learned was to work in a team with different backgrounds. For me personally it was something completly new to work in team-constellations with people that do not have the same cultural or study background as myself. I perosnally think this will be especially important for me in the future, when I will work in companies where different skill specific teams need to work together.

Receiving & Giving Feedback

Giving & Receiving feedback was something I've done my entire life, but to be honest it was more generic than really helpful. And I would also say that applies in terms of received feedback. So it was definitely good to learn on how give & receive feedback in a right way, which will also be important working on projects in the future.

Design Thinking

Although the 3 days bootcamp was sometimes quite exhausting, I think the principles of design thinking and the knowledge I got, will help me a lot when approaching projects in the future. It also gave me personally a whole new perspective on how to approach certain things I work on.

Business Planning

During my time in the iLab, I gained valuable insights into business planning. The bootcamp was great, providing a understanding of strategic development, financial forecasting, and market analysis. I learned a lot things, that might be useful for a project in my future developing career.

Agile Project Management

As I probably mentioned several times in this portfolio and during my time in the iLab, I enjoyed the Agile Project Management part the most. The project management was the biggest key aspect, for me taking part in the iLab. The reason is mainly that project management is basically key when you work in areas like Development etc. I really enjoyed getting to know more about it and practice it for the second half of the iLab. To conclude, I actually think this part of the iLab should get even more attention within the iLab itself (introducing it earlier etc.).

Refelections

Although I learned a lot during my time and experienced a lot of great things, I think they were also some moments of phases, that did not go quite well perosnally while working in the iLab.

Own performance

I perosnally think that I even could have given a lot more effort sometimes during my time in the iLab, but was affected by different factors. For me personally Gate 1 was kind of exhausting sometimes due to the topic, coach and team constellation. And I was more driven to work on a prototype or product for the project itself, rather than a lot of theoretical stuff.

After Gate 2, things got a lot better. I fitted pretty well into my new team and I finally could at least bring in some of my skills, but the motivation to give even more was sometimes lacking due to some, for me at least uninteresting appointments. But this totally something I need work on, so I need to work on my personal expectations or find some workarounds for that kind of situations.

Motivation

As I mentioned before, motivation was an issue for me, which afffected me in many ways. I kind of had way different expectations about the iLab, which resulted into high lows sometimes for me. Additionally it was sometimes hard to manage being in the iLab, taking a course at my home university, working and having a private life. My conclusion would be, that I need to improve my general daily structure and work life balance in general, so in the end everything gets done properly without me being exhausted and not motivated.
