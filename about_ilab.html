Interdisciplinary Lab – iLab

The iLab is a full time elective Semester programme for everyone to participate, no matter if you are a Bachelor or Master Degree Student. During the iLab you will work with people from many different disciplines and nations. Due to to that, english will be the working language over the whole semester. The main focus you work on during the iLab are challenges, which are relevant for todays society. Throughout the semester you will form your self-organised teams and develop a project that could already be a well working product.

Link to the official iLab Website

Facts about the iLab

Full-Time Elective Semester (30 ECTS credits)

You can complete the iLab instead of a regular semester within the framework of your mobility window.

Various Disciplines and Nations

You collaborate with students from various disciplines and diverse cultural backgrounds.

English as a Working Language

You consolidate and expand your linguistic skills in the English-language work setting.

Challenges Relevant for Society

You find solutions to complex current challenges (based on the SDGs).

Self-Organised Teams

You work with your team daily and in an autonomous manner.

Support from Lab Master and Coaches

You are accompanied and supported by professionals from the very beginning.

24/7 Offices

You can use designated office rooms with suitable equipment throughout the entire semester.

Future Skills

The real-life learning setting in the iLab allows you to acquire and strengthen important future skills.

The iLab Process

A generall overview of all important events, dates, lectures during the iLab process

  • Design Thinking Bootcamp

    In the Design Thinking Bootcamp, you will get to know in 3 whole days, how to deal with complex issues. This input will be very important throughtout the whole semester while you work on your challanges.

  • The first challenge

    After about 2 Weeks in the iLab, you will get your first challenge allocated. Additionally, you will have one team partner you will work with at least until the Gate 1.

  • Various Lectures

    During the whole Semester in the iLab you will get different inputs for the semester, your project or even for your personal skills:

    • Giving & Receiving Feedback
    • Storytelling
    • Pitching
    • Usability & Evaluation
    • Co-creating solutions from shared values
    • Intersectionality: An analytical tool in research
    • Calculate carbon footprint: pracitcal skills for climate change
    • Improvisation Workshop
    • Team-Building & team conflicts

  • Team Check-Ins

    During the Team-Check-Ins you and your group will come together with one of the Labmasters. During the the Team Check-In you will talk in general on how the project is progressing, are there certain issues within the team and so on.

  • Gate 1

    After roughly a month, you and your team partner will present your first results while working on your allocated challenge. Afterwards, a Jury, which consists for example of 3 lectures and the other teams, will vote on which challenge will continue to Gate 2.

  • Personal Goal Talks

    During the personal goal talks, you will talk a lot about your personal development during your time in the iLab. The main focus of these talks is to keep you on track to your predefined learning goals and also maybe to help your personal growth.

  • Business Planning Bootcamp

    In this 2 day business planning bootcamp, you will get to know everything about the basics of business. Contentwise you will learn what is important when you create a business and especially what needs to be considered.

  • Gate 2

    Roughly one month after the Gate 1, you will present your challenge again, but this time with a visual prototype and an idea, which could lead to a product. Compared to Gate 1, depending on the results, it is possible that you worked on a whole new challenge with a new group. The voting system will be the same as Gate 1.

  • Agile Project Management Bootcamp

    After the Gate 2, the first half of the iLab is done and the project development phase starts. In a 2 days bootcamp about agile project management, you and your group will learn how to plan your development phase. As the bootcamp continues, you will get to know different approaches of project management and how to organise your project.

    Additionally you will now work in sprints, sprint reviews, sprint plannings and retrospectives until the end of the semester.

    Sprint Schedule Cycle (2 Weeks = 1 Sprint):

    • Sprint planning
    • Sprint Review
    • Sprint Retrospective
    • ↪ back to sprint planning etc.

  • Project Vernissage

    Every wintersemester the FH St. Pölten has an event called the Project Vernissage. The Project Vernissage is a project fair and a chance to present your project to other students. Additionally you will also get the chance to pitch about your project. And in the end you might win a prize. But nonetheless most importantly, you might get important feedback that will improve your project in the last few steps in the iLab.

  • Final Presentation

    After a whole semester, you and your group will present your final results of your project.
